Connect to Nogrammerly

This add-in checks your writing with the Nogrammerly app on this PC. Nothing you write leaves the PC.

  1. Click the ✦ icon near the clock.
  2. Choose Add-ins for other apps › Let add-ins on this PC use Nogrammerly.
  3. Choose Copy add-in key from the same menu, then paste it here.
